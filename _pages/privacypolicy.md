# Privacy Policy

iProTV is developed and published by an independent developer ("we", "the developer"). This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

## The short version
iProTV has no account system and no server of its own. Your playlists, credentials and viewing history stay on your device and in your personal iCloud account: we never see them. To fix bugs and improve the app, we receive anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. We don't sell or share any data.

## Content
iProTV does not provide, host, or distribute any media content. You configure the app with your own playlist sources (M3U or Xtream-Codes portals). You are responsible for having the right to access the content you configure.

## Data stored on your device and in your iCloud
- Playlist sources and credentials (Xtream login, M3U URLs)
- Favorites, watch progress, and app settings

This data is stored locally and, if iCloud is enabled, synced through your private iCloud account via Apple's CloudKit. It is never transmitted to the developer. If you are not signed in to iCloud, no data leaves your device for syncing.

## Crash reports and usage statistics
Both services host their data in the European Union:
- **Sentry** (Functional Software, Inc.) — technical reports
- **TelemetryDeck** (TelemetryDeck GmbH) — anonymous usage statistics

This data is anonymous and used only to improve the app.

## Other services the app contacts
- **Apple** — purchases and subscriptions; we never receive your payment information
- **TMDB** — artwork and metadata
- **SkipDB, IntroDB and TheIntroDB** — intro skipping
- **OpenSubtitles** — subtitles, if you sign in
- **YouTube** — trailers
- **Our website** — news and announcements
- **Your playlist provider** — streams and TV guide, requested directly from the servers you configured

## What we don't do
- No ads, no tracking, no sale of data
- No account required, no personal data collected by the developer

## Your rights
You can contact us at any time about your data. As crash reports and statistics are anonymous, we cannot link them to a specific person.

## Children
The app is not directed at children.

We may update this policy; the current version is always available at this URL.
